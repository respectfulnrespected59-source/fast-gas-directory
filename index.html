03 / How it works

Three steps.
No guessing.

A directory's only job is to get you to the right counter with the right paperwork. These are the three things that actually decide whether your trip is wasted.

Check the program

Enter a ZIP. Fast Gas resolves it to a state and tells you whether that state runs adult-use retail, a medical-patient program, a low-THC program, or nothing at all.

Filter to your needs

Narrow by delivery, open hours, ADA access or verification status. In medical-only states the listings say so, because a recreational walk-in is not an option there.

Bring the right ID

Adult-use needs a valid government-issued ID showing 21+. Medical programs need your state patient credential. Confirm current rules with the regulator before you travel.

04 / Clear by design

What this is.
What it is not.

There are no invented ratings on real businesses, no press quotes and no borrowed customer claims. Trust starts with saying plainly what you are looking at.

Sample listings, labelled

Business names in this directory are fictional and generated. Cities, ZIP codes and state programs are real. Nothing here claims to describe an actual storefront.

A snapshot, not legal advice

Program statuses reflect a point in time and change with legislation and licensing. Each state card names its regulator so you can verify the current rule at the source.

21+ throughout

This concept is built for adults 21 and over. Medical-only states are marked separately because they require a state-issued patient credential, not just age.

Nothing leaves your browser

There is no cart, no checkout and no backend. Searches and filters run locally; the forms validate and then tell you plainly that nothing was sent or stored.

05 / Questions

The honest FAQ.

Short answers, including the ones that are inconvenient.

Are these real dispensaries?

No. Every business name is fictional and deterministically generated, so the directory can demonstrate real search, filtering and distance behaviour without putting words in a real company's mouth. The cities, ZIP codes and state program statuses are real.

Can I order from this site?

No. There is no cart, checkout, payment processor or backend of any kind. The forms validate your input and then confirm that nothing was transmitted or stored.

How does the ZIP lookup cover all 50 states?

It uses the public USPS allocation of three-digit ZIP prefixes to states, so any valid five-digit US ZIP resolves - roughly sixty ranges instead of a list of forty thousand codes. Entering a ZIP in a state with no program tells you exactly that, rather than showing an empty result list.

Is the legal status information current?

Treat it as a snapshot, not a source of truth. Cannabis law moves quickly and licensing changes faster. Every state entry names the responsible regulator precisely so you can confirm the current rule before relying on it. This is not legal advice.

Why do some states show no listings at all?

Because they have no legal retail dispensaries to list. States with low-THC or CBD-only programs, and states with no program, appear in the state grid with their status but carry no storefront listings. Inventing listings there would be the dishonest option.
