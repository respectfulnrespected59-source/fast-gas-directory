03 / How it works

Three steps.
No guessing.

A directory's only job is to get you to the right counter with the right paperwork. These are the three things that actually decide whether your trip is wasted.

Check the program

Enter a ZIP. Fast Gas resolves it to a state and tells you whether that state runs adult-use retail, a medical-patient program, a low-THC program, or nothing at all.

Filter to your needs

Narrow by delivery, open hours, ADA access or verification status. In medical-only states the listings say so, because a recreational walk-in is not an option there.

Bring the right ID

Adult-use needs a valid government-issued ID showing 21+. Medical programs need your state patient credential. Confirm current rules with the regulator before you travel.

04 / Clear by design

Real licences, named sources

Every retailer is a currently-active licence pulled from a state regulator's public register. Each state card names its source and the date it was refreshed, so you can check any entry at source.

A snapshot, not legal advice

No invented signals

You will find no star ratings, opening hours or prices here. Regulators do not publish them, and inventing them about a named business would misdirect you to a closed or wrong door. Call ahead.

05 / Questions

The honest FAQ.

Short answers, including the ones that are inconvenient.

Are these real dispensaries?

Yes. Every listing is a currently-active retail licence taken from a state regulator's public register, with the licence number shown on the card so you can verify it at source. An earlier version of this site used generated sample listings; that data has been removed entirely.

Why are there no ratings, hours or prices?

Because regulators do not publish them. A star rating invented about a named real business is defamatory, and an invented opening time sends you to a locked door. Where we do not have a fact, we show nothing and tell you to call ahead.

Which states are covered?

Only the states listed in the coverage panel at the top. Each state regulator publishes its register differently - some offer a daily API, some a downloadable file, some only a search page - so states are added one verified source at a time. An uncovered state says so plainly instead of showing an empty list you might read as "none here".

Does an active licence mean the shop is open?

No. It means the state lists the licence as active on the refresh date shown. A business can hold a licence while closed for refurbishment, between premises, or not yet trading. Always call.

Is the legal status information current?

Treat programme status as a snapshot, not a source of truth. Cannabis law moves quickly. Every state entry names its regulator so you can confirm before relying on it. This is not legal advice.

Can I order from this site?

No. There is no cart, checkout or payment of any kind. The directory points you at a licensed door; everything after that happens between you and the retailer.
